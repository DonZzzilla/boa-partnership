# Executive Brief: BOA Partnership Proposal

**Prepared by:** DonZzzilla (on behalf of the Ghost of Tabor community)
**Date:** June 2026
**Purpose:** To outline the current state of the Battlefield Operation and Awareness (BOA), identify key challenges the guild is navigating, and propose a collaborative partnership to strengthen mutual outcomes.

---

## Current State of BOA

The Battlefield Operation and Awareness (BOA) has long served as a cornerstone organization for the Ghost of Tabor player community. Through volunteer-driven mentorship, structured onboarding programs, educational initiatives, wiki maintenance, documentation efforts, and broad community support, BOA has played an instrumental role in helping players navigate the game and become productive, engaged members of the community. BOA's culture of selfless contribution is a significant asset—one that has shaped the experience of countless players and continues to underpin the health of the broader ecosystem.

Today, BOA faces a period of transition. While the guild's mission remains as relevant as ever, several converging factors require thoughtful attention and proactive investment to ensure operational continuity and long-term sustainability.

## Leadership Stability

In recent months, BOA has experienced notable departures from its leadership ranks. Several senior volunteers and officers—individuals who have historically driven guild operations, mentorship pipelines, and community engagement—have moved on due to personal commitments, shifting availability, or natural transitions. While these departures are understandable, they have created gaps in institutional capacity at a time when evolving community needs demand experienced stewardship.

**Opportunity:** A partnership focused on structured leadership pipelines and succession planning can help BOA build resilience against future transitions, ensuring that critical roles are always supported by capable successors.

## Volunteer Retention

Volunteers are the lifeblood of BOA. Their contributions—ranging from one-on-one mentorship to documentation and event coordination—represent an enormous investment of time and expertise. However, volunteer communities across gaming guilds face well-documented retention challenges: burnout, lack of recognition, unclear pathways for growth, and competing real-world priorities.

BOA's volunteer base, while dedicated, is not immune to these dynamics. Proactive strategies to recognize contributions, provide meaningful development opportunities, and sustain engagement will be essential for retaining the talent that powers BOA's mission.

**Opportunity:** Partnering on volunteer management best practices—including recognition frameworks, burnout prevention, and skills-based role assignments—can meaningfully improve retention and morale.

## Geographic Coverage and Responsiveness

Ghost of Tabor's player base is international, with significant populations in European Union time zones. As BOA's volunteer roster has shifted, gaps have emerged in coverage during EU prime-time hours. This means that a meaningful segment of the player community may not have timely access to mentorship, guidance, or community support when they need it most.

**Opportunity:** A targeted initiative to recruit and empower EU-based volunteers—supported by flexible scheduling and clear role definitions—would close this coverage gap and ensure that BOA's services are truly accessible to the full player community.

## Institutional Knowledge Retention

Perhaps the most pressing concern is the risk of knowledge loss. BOA's accumulated expertise—documented and undocumented—represents years of institutional learning about community management, player onboarding, game mechanics, and organizational processes. When experienced volunteers depart without structured handover, that knowledge is often irretrievably lost.

While BOA maintains wiki pages and documentation, these resources require ongoing curation to remain accurate and useful. Without a deliberate knowledge-retention strategy, each leadership transition risks resetting hard-won organizational progress.

**Opportunity:** A partnership initiative to systematize knowledge capture—through documented processes, training materials, and regular knowledge-transfer protocols—would protect BOA's institutional memory and accelerate onboarding for new volunteers.

## Desired Outcomes

This partnership proposal is grounded in a collaborative, forward-looking vision. Together, we aim to:

- **Strengthen leadership continuity** by building structured succession pipelines and development pathways for emerging leaders.
- **Improve volunteer retention** through recognition, engagement, and burnout-prevention strategies.
- **Ensure full geographic coverage**, with particular attention to EU time-zone responsiveness.
- **Preserve institutional knowledge** with systematic documentation and handover practices.
- **Sustain and scale BOA's core mission** of mentorship, education, and community support for Ghost of Tabor players.

These outcomes serve not only BOA's interests but the broader health and vibrancy of the Ghost of Tabor community. By investing in BOA's organizational resilience today, we ensure that the guild can continue to fulfill its vital role for years to come.

---

*This brief is intended to open a constructive dialogue. We view every challenge outlined above as an opportunity for collaborative growth, and we look forward to discussing how a partnership can drive meaningful, measurable impact.*
