## Common Scenarios

**Q: I'm running 10+ minutes late. What do I do?**
A: Text the Volunteer Coordinator at [number] immediately. Do not simply
arrive late without notice — it impacts the team.

**Q: I don't know how to do a task I've been assigned.**
A: Ask! Never guess. Flag your supervisor or any team lead. We'd rather
clarify than redo work.

**Q: An attendee has a complaint or is upset.**
A: Listen politely, empathize, and escalate to the Event Coordinator.
Do not attempt to resolve conflicts independently.

**Q: The weather changes unexpectedly (outdoor event).**
A: Follow the Event Coordinator's directions. The event-specific
contingency plan is posted at [location].

## Template Usage Guidelines

| Template | Use For | Tone | Length |
|----------|---------|------|--------|
| **SOP** | Any repeatable process across events, finance, communications, partnerships, or HR | Formal, precise, structured | 2–5 pages |
| **Onboarding Page** | Partner, volunteer, or staff orientation content | Warm yet professional, welcoming, clear | 3–6 pages |
| **Volunteer Role Guide** | Individual volunteer position descriptions and shift instructions | Action-oriented, scannable, encouraging | 1–2 pages |

### Writing Standards Across All Templates

1. **Active voice** — "Complete the checklist" not "The checklist should be completed."
2. **Specific timelines** — "Arrive 2 hours before event start" not "Arrive early."
3. **Named owners** — Always assign a person or role, never leave ownership ambiguous.
4. **Checklists over paragraphs** — When describing procedures, use checklists whenever possible.
5. **Link liberally** — Cross-reference related SOPs, FAQs, and training modules.
6. **Version everything** — Every template includes version, date, and owner fields.
7. **Accessibility**   Use descriptive link text, sufficient heading hierarchy, and alt text for any images.
