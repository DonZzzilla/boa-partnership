## 🎯 Key Talking Points

1. **BOA has grown 3×** in two years — infrastructure hasn't kept pace
2. **35% 12-month retention** (down from 48%) — recognition programs improve this by 30-40%
3. **642-876 hours/year** can be saved through automation (BOA Bot)
4. **78% of volunteers** say more recognition would increase satisfaction
5. **Only 25% of decisions** are documented — knowledge base eliminates this risk
6. **EU coverage gaps** from leadership departures — targeted recruitment closes them
7. **150+ events, 50+ leaders mentored** — BOA's track record speaks for itself

## 🗣️ Recommended Discussion Order

1. **Open** with BOA's track record and appreciation (Slide 1-4)
2. **Present challenges** honestly — as opportunities, not complaints (Slide 5)
3. **Lead with Recognition Program** — highest approval likelihood, near-zero cost
4. **Introduce Knowledge Base** — quick win, high ROI
5. **Present BOA Bot** — medium-term flagship, frame as force multiplier
6. **Discuss Communication** — technical transparency as trust-building
7. **Close with Vision** — phased approach, co-designed partnership

---

## ⚠️ Tone Guidelines

- **Professional, respectful, collaborative** — never demanding
- **Data-driven** — use specific numbers and examples
- **Frame as opportunities** — not grievances or requests
- **"We" and "together"** — not "we need" or "you should"
- **Honest about challenges** — transparency builds trust
- **Be human** — this is a community conversation, not a boardroom pitch
