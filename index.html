// 02 - THREAT ASSESSMENT

Challenges Identified

We believe in transparency. These are organizational risks we have identified.

▶ Volunteer Burnout CRITICAL

41% of departing volunteers cite burnout. Repetitive manual tasks consume time that skilled volunteers could spend on high-impact work.

▶ Knowledge Retention HIGH

When experienced volunteers depart, institutional memory walks out the door. Only 25% of decisions are documented.

▶ Operational Burden MEDIUM

35% of volunteer hours are spent on repetitive, automatable tasks. Monthly reporting takes ~20 person-hours.

▶ EU Coverage Gap MEDIUM

Leadership departures created responsiveness gaps during EU prime-time hours.

// 05 - COMMS OPPORTUNITIES

Sharing Knowledge, Building Trust

Meaningful opportunities to strengthen community trust through technical transparency.

Technical Information Sharing

BOA deep community expertise gives us unique insight into Ghost of Tabor systems. Sharing more about core mechanics benefits everyone.

● Reduce Speculation

Authoritative information from trusted sources reduces misinformation and frustration.

● Player Education

Understanding game mechanics helps players make informed decisions and enjoy the game more deeply.

● Community Trust

Transparency signals respect. Organizations that share knowledge build stronger loyalty.

● Collaborative Discovery

When players understand the why behind systems, they provide better feedback.

Our approach: We are not asking for access to proprietary systems. Community-facing documentation of general mechanics would be a meaningful step forward.
